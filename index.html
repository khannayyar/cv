About Me

I am a Faculty of Computer Science in the Department of Computer Science at Shaqra University, College of Computing and Information Technology. With a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from India, my research expertise lies in network security, cloud computing, and intrusion detection systems.

My doctoral work focused on developing innovative network intrusion management models to prevent web form spamming in cloud computing environments. I am deeply committed to advancing knowledge through cutting-edge research, inspiring the next generation of scholars, and leveraging technology to address real-world security challenges in cloud-based applications.
